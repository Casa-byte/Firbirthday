a letter, because a caption was never going to be enough

dear twin,

I started this letter six times, and every version sounded like a greeting card, u would have seen through it in one second, so I'm just going to talk to u the way I always do. Only with better lighting.

Let's start with ur smile, people say "a smile that lights up the room," but that's too loud for yours, yours is quieter, It's more like morning reaching a window!! u can't say when it arrived, u only notice that the room is softer and u're okay. I'm serious when I say it deserves protection. If smiles had bodyguards I'd apply for the job, and be terrible at it, because I'd be too busy smiling back.

And ur kindness. It's how u listen & remember the thing that's been bothering someone and ask about it several days later. It's how u make people feel like they're not too much. tbh, Kebaikanmu itu bukan yang heboh firr, it's the steady kind. The kind that shows up and stays. I've watched u be gentle with people who didn't always earn it, and I'll admit I've wanted to be a little less gentle on ur behalf.

Blue is your colour and I refuse to believe that's an accident. Blue is the sea on a day with no wind. It's the sky just before evening. It's what you are to me: the quiet in the middle of a loud week. When everything is spinning, I think of you and my shoulders drop an inch.

And of course it's lilies. Not roses begging to be noticed. Lilies flower that's tall, soft, a little elegant, never trying. u're a lily person all the way down fir. Soft, but not fragile, and y'know the difference better than anyone.

Now, the fish. I need the record to show that I have seen ur face when u find a cute fish keychain. u go from "normal functioning human" to "tiny scientist who has discovered something sacred" in 0.3 seconds, If I ever can't find u, I'll check the nearest fish merchandise first. Most reliable tracking system in the world and penguins too, because u love creatures who dress formally and still fall over. Honestly? Relatable. They also walk through the coldest places on earth huddled together so nobody gets left out. I think that's why u like 'em.

Then there's matcha. I've accepted that every plan we make has a matcha stop built in, like a pit stop for your emotional stability. A little bitter, a little sweet, quietly comforting. Frankly it's you in a cup, and please don't repeat that to the barista.

And Charles Leclerc. I'm not going to say anything about the team's strategy calls, because I'm a good friend and you've suffered enough, but u taught me what being a fan really is.. believing, lap after lap, that things will turn around. "Iya, nanti pasti naik kok." I've started using that energy on everything good I hope for u. Also, if the pit wall ever needs someone to shout at it, I know a person who would do it politely, and then cry a little.

We don't share blood, we don't share a face, but we share a wavelength :> the same chaos, the same sentences started by one and finished by the other. Aku nggak pernah kepikiran bakal nemu seseorang yang rasanya kayak rumah tanpa harus jadi keluarga di atas kertas. Tapi ternyata bisa and its u!!

So I'm not going to wish u anything enormous today. I just hope ur day is soft enough for u.. matcha in your hand, a fish keychain in ur pocket, a good race somewhere in the background, and a message from me when u least expect it. Pelan-pelan aja, 'kayy. You don't need to be anything more than u already are, i always proud of ur every progress

Consider this a very long hug in text form. Happy birthday, twin. Love yaa, in the most twin way possible.

— Alic ♡
