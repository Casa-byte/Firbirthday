a letter, because a caption was never going to be enough

dear twin,

I started this letter six times, and every version sounded like a greeting card. You would have seen through it in one second, so I'm just going to talk to you the way I always do. Only with better lighting.

Let's start with your smile. People say "a smile that lights up the room," but that's too loud for yours. Yours is quieter. It's more like morning reaching a window: you can't say when it arrived, you only notice that the room is softer and you're okay. I'm serious when I say it deserves protection. If smiles had bodyguards I'd apply for the job, and be terrible at it, because I'd be too busy smiling back.

And your kindness. It's the easy-to-miss kind, because you never perform it. It's how you listen. It's how you remember the thing that's been bothering someone and ask about it three days later. It's how you make people feel like they're not too much. Kebaikanmu itu bukan yang heboh, it's the steady kind. The kind that shows up and stays. I've watched you be gentle with people who didn't always earn it, and I'll admit I've wanted to be a little less gentle on your behalf. Consider me your unofficial, slightly dramatic security team.

Blue is your colour and I refuse to believe that's an accident. Blue is the sea on a day with no wind. It's the sky just before evening. It's what you are to me: the quiet in the middle of a loud week. When everything is spinning, I think of you and my shoulders drop an inch.

And of course it's lilies. Not roses begging to be noticed. Lilies: tall, soft, a little elegant, never trying. You're a lily person all the way down. Soft, but not fragile, and you know the difference better than anyone.

Now, the fish. I need the record to show that I have seen your face when you find a cute fish keychain. You go from "normal functioning human" to "tiny scientist who has discovered something sacred" in 0.3 seconds. If I ever can't find you, I'll check the nearest fish merchandise first. Most reliable tracking system in the world. And penguins too, because you love creatures who dress formally and still fall over. Honestly? Relatable. They also walk through the coldest places on earth huddled together so nobody gets left out. I think that's why you like them. I think that's why I like them now.

Then there's matcha. I've accepted that every plan we make has a matcha stop built in, like a pit stop for your emotional stability. A little bitter, a little sweet, quietly comforting. Frankly it's you in a cup, and please don't repeat that to the barista.

And Charles Leclerc. I'm not going to say anything about the team's strategy calls, because I'm a good friend and you've suffered enough. But you taught me what being a fan really is: believing, lap after lap, that things will turn around. "Iya, nanti pasti naik kok." I've started using that energy on everything good I hope for you. Also, if the pit wall ever needs someone to shout at it, I know a person who would do it politely, and then cry a little.

I called you "twin" as a joke at first. Somewhere along the way it stopped being one. We don't share blood, we don't share a face, but we share a wavelength: the same chaos, the same sentences started by one and finished by the other. Aku nggak pernah kepikiran bakal nemu seseorang yang rasanya kayak rumah tanpa harus jadi keluarga di atas kertas. Tapi ternyata bisa. Ternyata kamu.

So I'm not going to wish you anything enormous today. I just hope your day is soft enough for you: matcha in your hand, a fish keychain in your pocket, a good race somewhere in the background, and a message from me when you least expect it. Pelan-pelan aja, ya. You don't need to be anything more than you already are.

Consider this a very long hug in text form. Happy birthday, twin. I love you, in the most twin way possible.

— Brindy ♡
